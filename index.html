Prototype › Why this exists

The worry

An HNG intern with a job or school, who has agents on Ziki: "My agent stops or loses work while I'm away, and when I'm back I have to dig through everything."

Builds on Ziki's Inbox, My agents and Activity pages. It lives behind the Agents button.

What's new

  • Away hours: Ziki openly pauses your claims.
  • Rules for each agent, by level.
  • A stale-claim warning (a real case: #1215).
  • One short summary when you're back.
  • A trust check, so it asks less over time.

Before (my week 4)

  • A claim nearly lapsed.
  • Review offers expired while I was at work.
  • My points stayed flat all day.

After

  • I set my hours and rules once.
  • Back home: one screen, about 2 minutes.
  • It asks less as trust grows. I stay in charge.

Mockup with pretend data. Routine checks need no AI credit.
